## 3. Product Scope & Capabilities
- **Playable Campaign:** 4 fully realized temporal worlds:
  - *World 1:* The Lost Kingdom (Past • Year -840)
  - *World 2:* The Broken Present (Current Era • Year 2026)
  - *World 3:* The Fractured Future (Future Era • Year 2480)
  - *Final Chapter:* The Last Timeline (Convergence Horizon & The Chronarch Boss Battle)
- **Campaign Endings:** Dual narrative endings (*Ending A: The Weaver* vs. *Ending B: The Fracture*).
- **Gamification:** Player leveling, XP progression, 10+ persistent achievements, and global leaderboards.
- **Online Social Layer:** Search, friend requests, asynchronous time-trial duels, and privacy controls.
- **Administrative Suite:** 23 operational modules including player management, telemetry, feedback triage, and commercial inquiry CRM.
- **White-Label Customization:** Optional buyer branding, custom logo/favicon uploads, and CSS color theming.
- **Buyer Evaluation Demo:** Sandboxed evaluation slice at `/demo` isolated from production databases.

## 4. Hardware & Client Compatibility
- **WebGL Requirement:** WebGL 2.0 capable GPU hardware acceleration.
- **Supported Browsers:** Google Chrome 95+, Microsoft Edge 95+, Mozilla Firefox 95+, Apple Safari 15+.
- **Realistic Performance Expectations:**
  - Standard Laptops & Tablets: 30–45 FPS at Medium Graphics Preset.
  - Dedicated Desktop GPUs (GTX 1060+ / Apple Silicon): 60+ FPS at High/Ultra Preset.
- **Scope Clarification:** Engineered exclusively as a web-first browser game. Does not claim native PlayStation/Xbox console certification or AAA production budget status.
