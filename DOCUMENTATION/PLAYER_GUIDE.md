# AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME
## OPERATIVE FIELD MANUAL (PLAYER GUIDE)

Welcome to *AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME*. This guide covers controls, mechanics, progression, and social features to help you navigate the multiverse.

---

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Create an Account:**
   - Head to `/register` and choose your explorer callsign, email, and password.
2. **Access Your Dashboard:**
   - Log in to access your personal Operative Portal (`/player/dashboard`), where you can review your current level, unlocked achievements, active world, and story journal.
3. **Launch the Game:**
   - Click **"Resume 3D Game"** or navigate to `/play` to launch the 3D game canvas.

---

## 2. Controls & Traversal

### Desktop Keyboard & Mouse
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / A / S / D** | Walk & Maneuver character |
| **Shift (Hold)** | Sprint (Consumes Chrono Energy for double speed) |
| **Spacebar** | Jump / Vault over obstacles |
| **Mouse Drag** | Orbit third-person camera |
| **E** | Interact with terminals, pedestals, and portals |
| **J** | Open Story Journal and Lore Archives |
| **Esc / P** | Pause game and access settings |

### Mobile Touch Controls
- **Virtual Joystick (Bottom Left):** Touch and drag to move character in any direction.
- **Sprint Button (Bottom Right):** Tap to toggle sprint mode.
- **Jump Button (Bottom Right):** Tap to jump or leap across gaps.
- **Interact Button (Floating):** Appears when near interactive terminals, cores, or pedestals.
- **Touch Camera:** Swipe anywhere on the screen background to orbit the camera.

## 3. World Objectives & Puzzles

- **World 1 — The Lost Kingdom:**
  - Search the moonlit castle ruins to gather 3 Temporal Energy Fragments.
  - Interact with the central pedestal to decipher the ancient rune puzzle.
  - Unlock the stone gateway and step onto the portal dais.
- **World 2 — The Broken Present:**
  - Locate the damaged highway terminal to access the city network.
  - Navigate rain-soaked alleys to harvest 3 Quantum Energy Cores.
  - Enter the underground laboratory and repair the Central Time Core reactor.
- **World 3 — The Fractured Future:**
  - Activate the Phase Bridge to cross the floating sky islands.
  - Collect 3 Chrono Shards and calibrate the 3 Time Labyrinth resonator switches.
  - Confront **THE CHRONARCH** in the Spire Arena. Dodge orbital energy shields and strike vulnerable core nodes!
- **World 4 — The Last Timeline (The Zero Horizon):**
  - Walk the celestial bridge to the Nexus of Creation.
  - Synchronize the 3 Epoch Keystones.
  - Choose reality's destiny:
    - **Ending A:** Stitch all timelines into one unified continuum.
    - **Ending B:** Set all timelines free as an infinite multiverse.

## 4. Progression & Customization

- **Earning XP:** Earn XP by completing worlds, solving environmental puzzles, finding hidden lore, and winning asynchronous player challenges.
- **Ranks:**
  - Level 1: Novice Explorer
  - Level 2: Chrono Initiate
  - Level 3: Rift Walker
  - Level 4: Epoch Seeker
  - Level 5: Time Weaver
  - Level 6: Continuum Master
  - Level 7: Singularity Adept
  - Level 8: Singularity Master
- **Avatar Customization:** Choose from 6 distinct avatar holograms in your Profile settings (`/player/profile`).

---

## 5. Social Hub & Player Challenges

Navigate to `/social` to access the optional online multiplayer layer:
- **Friends Roster:** Send friend requests to fellow operatives by username.
- **Asynchronous Challenges:** Issue speedrun or harvest challenges to friends. Complete your run on your own schedule; the server automatically verifies telemetry and awards bonus XP to the victor!
- **Privacy Controls:** You maintain total control over your operative data. Toggle public profile visibility, achievement showcases, and leaderboard indexing at any time in `/social/privacy`.
- **Graphics Settings:** On lower-spec hardware or mobile devices, open the Pause Menu (`Esc`) and set Graphics Quality to **Medium** or **Low** for butter-smooth frame rates.
