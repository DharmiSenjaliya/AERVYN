# AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME — Buyer Feature List & Deliverables Inventory

This document details all systems, deliverables, and engineering capabilities included in the commercial release of **AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME**.

---

## 1. 3D Game Engine & Rendering Systems
- **Three.js & React Three Fiber Runtime:** High-performance WebGL 2.0 rendering at up to 60+ FPS.
- **Third-Person Locomotion Controller:** Smooth acceleration, sprint velocity curve, jumping trajectory, and responsive orientation.
- **Dynamic Orbital Camera:** Orbit-tracking camera with collision damping and pitch constraints.
- **Procedural 3D Environment Geometry:** Ancient temples, ruined city skyscrapers, cybernetic platforms, and celestial islands built procedurally without bulky external binary dependencies.
- **Dynamic Lighting & Shaders:** Directional sunlight, ambient radiance, glowing emissive runes, and temporal portal vortex shaders.
- **Particle System Emitters:** Dust motes, quantum energy sparks, dimensional portal vortexes, and checkpoint activation bursts.

---

## 2. Narrative Campaign & Game World Content
- **World 1 — The Lost Kingdom:** Ancient past sanctuary, runic pedestal dial puzzle, Keystone recovery, and gateway portal.
- **World 2 — The Broken Present:** Modern urban ruins, electrified obstacles, circuit breaker puzzle, and subway transit tunnels.
- **World 3 — The Fractured Future:** Cyber citadel, laser defense grids, quantum frequency alignment puzzle, and patrolling security drones.
- **Final Chapter — The Last Timeline:** Celestial event horizon, multi-phase boss fight against **The Chronarch**, and dual branching endings:
  - *Ending A: Universal Restoration*
  - *Ending B: Infinite Divergence*

---

## 3. Web Audio Synthesis Architecture
- **Procedural Web Audio API Sound Generator:**
  - Ambient timeline drones (calm forest, urban hum, cybernetic sub-bass, cosmic choir).
  - Physical interaction SFX (footsteps, jump exertion, ground impact).
  - Puzzle feedback (rune dial rotation ticks, circuit breaker clicks, laser frequency hums).
  - Dimensional portal resonance.
  - Boss combat telegraph stingers and hit reactions.
- **100% Royalty-Free:** Zero third-party audio licensing encumbrances or royalty liabilities.

---

## 4. Player Platform & Gamification Portal
- **Player Gaming Dashboard:** Level progression, XP bar, total playtime, worlds conquered, and active missions.
- **Inventory System:** Chrono Keystones, timeline fragments, runic relics, and tool consumables.
- **Achievements & Badges System:** 10+ unlockable achievements with tier icons, timestamps, and cloud sync.
- **Global & Social Leaderboards:** Ranked by completion time and total accumulated timeline XP.
- **Story Journal:** Lore entries unlocked across timeline exploration.

---

## 5. Online Social Layer & Community Features
- **Public Player Discovery:** Search players by username with debounce.
- **Friend Management:** Send, accept, decline, and cancel mutual friendship links.
- **Asynchronous Time-Trial Challenges:** Challenge friends to beat your world clear times.
- **Granular Privacy Controls:** Configurable public/friends-only/private profile and leaderboard visibility.
- **Social Moderation Controls:** Flag abusive user behavior and manage suspensions via admin panel.

---

## 6. Back-Office Admin Operations Suite (23 Modules)
1. **Executive Dashboard:** Real-time KPI counters (active players, sessions, completions, new inquiries).
2. **Player Accounts:** Search, view, edit, ban, or reset player profiles.
3. **Social Moderation:** Review player reports, inspect disputes, and execute account sanctions.
4. **Game Worlds:** Inspect and configure world parameters and difficulty.
5. **Levels & Missions:** Mission objective trackers and prerequisite maps.
6. **Characters & Enemies:** Encounter stats, health pools, and behavior attributes.
7. **Items, Inventory & Collectibles:** Catalog inspection and live inventory monitors.
8. **Achievements Engine:** Inspect unlock criteria and player reward distributions.
9. **Media & Assets:** Asset registry with write-protection for official branding.
10. **Game Sessions:** Live and historical player connection telemetry.
11. **Feedback & Support:** Direct feedback ticket management.
12. **Analytics & Reports:** Graphical charts and CSV export capabilities.
13. **Buyer Inquiries Management:** Dedicated CRM module for prospective licensing and buyout leads.
14. **System & Security Settings:** Rate limiting parameters, maintenance mode, and credentials.
