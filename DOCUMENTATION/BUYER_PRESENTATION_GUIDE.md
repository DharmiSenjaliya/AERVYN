## 1. Executive Presentation Best Practices

When presenting AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME to publishers, venture funds, gaming portals, or enterprise clients, adhere to the following delivery protocol:

### Preparation (10 Minutes Prior to Call)
1. **Launch Environment:**
   - Open Chrome or Firefox in Fullscreen (`F11`).
   - Navigate to `/buyer-demo`.
   - If presenting to a specific client (e.g. Apex Interactive), generate a custom private link at `/admin/buyer-demo` beforehand and open `/buyer-demo?token=<token>` so their company name appears in the top VIP badge.
2. **Audio Setup:**
   - Enable computer sound sharing. The Web Audio synthesizer generates dynamic harmonic chimes when portals activate.
3. **Hardware Acceleration:**
   - Verify hardware acceleration is active in browser settings to ensure a smooth 60 FPS canvas frame rate.
