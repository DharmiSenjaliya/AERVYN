# AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME
## 16-Section Guided Product Tour Specification
**Document Version:** 1.0.0 Commercial Release  
**Component:** `frontend/src/pages/buyer-demo/GuidedTour.jsx`  
**Target Audience:** Sales Engineers, Product Managers, Evaluating Executives

---

## 1. Overview

The **Guided Product Tour** is a sequential 16-section interactive walkthrough designed to demonstrate every facet of the commercial package in under 10 minutes.

Evaluators can access the tour directly from `/buyer-demo` by clicking **START GUIDED TOUR**.

---

## 3. Navigation Controls & Hotkeys

- **Next Section:** Click `Next` or press `ArrowRight` on keyboard.
- **Previous Section:** Click `Previous` or press `ArrowLeft` on keyboard.
- **Presentation Mode:** Toggle fullscreen presentation view using the Maximize/Minimize icon.
- **Exit Tour:** Click the `X` button or press `Escape` on keyboard.
- **Direct Section Jump:** Click any of the 16 step dots on the bottom navigation bar.
- **Skip to Quote:** Jump directly to Section 16 for immediate commercial inquiry submission.
- **Reduced Motion:** Dedicated toggle for evaluators sensitive to rapid animation transitions.
