# AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME — Commercial Product Overview

## Executive Summary
**AERVYN: ECHOES OF TIME** is a production-ready, commercial 3D web action-adventure and puzzle game. Engineered for direct browser execution using WebGL 2.0, Three.js, React 19, and Laravel 12, AERVYN delivers an engaging single-player experience with zero downloads, emulators, or native client installations required.

The game is packaged as a complete turnkey intellectual property and software asset suitable for:
1. **Commercial Asset Buyout:** Complete source acquisition with full intellectual property and distribution rights.
2. **B2B Web Licensing:** White-label deployment on gaming portals, telecom portals, and digital subscription services.
3. **Branded Reskinning & Enterprise Activations:** Tailoring the 3D environments, narratives, and collectibles for corporate storytelling, marketing activations, or educational initiatives.

## Core Gameplay Narrative
When an intrepid traveler discovers the dormant *Temporal Keystone* within ancient ruins, the device's activation inadvertently fragments spacetime into four unstable eras:
1. **The Lost Kingdom (Year -840):** An ancient stone sanctuary overgrown with mystical flora, where the first Chrono Keystone was fashioned.
2. **The Broken Present (Year 2026):** A modern metropolis fractured by temporal rifts, filled with electrified obstacles and corrupted junction boxes.
3. **The Fractured Future (Year 2480):** A neon-lit cybernetic platform suspended over an energy abyss, defended by security drones and laser grids.
4. **The Last Timeline (Convergence Horizon):** Floating celestial platforms at the edge of the continuum where the player confronts **The Chronarch** and chooses reality's ultimate fate.
